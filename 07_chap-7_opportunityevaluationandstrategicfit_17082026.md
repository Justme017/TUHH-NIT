## 7.1 Introduction

This chapter applies the structured scoring framework developed in **Chapter 3** to evaluate and prioritize the five highest-priority business opportunities identified in **Chapter 6**, using the regulatory classification and cost/timeline data established in **Chapter 5**. Each opportunity is scored across four dimensions:

| Dimension                 | Weight | What It Measures                                                                      | Primary Source       |
| ------------------------- | -----: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Market Attractiveness** |    35% | Market size, growth, pain-point severity, competitive intensity, reimbursement        | Chapter 4            |
| **Technical Feasibility** |    25% | Technology readiness, integration complexity, manufacturing scalability, IP landscape | Chapter 2, Chapter 6 |
| **Regulatory Complexity** |    20% | Device classification, clinical evidence needs, approval timeline, post-market burden | **Chapter 5**        |
| **Strategic Fit**         |    20% | Alignment with D.Med capabilities, portfolio synergy, resources, time to market       | Chapter 6            |

This chapter addresses **Research Question 3 (RQ3)**: _Considering market potential, technical feasibility, and regulatory requirements, which biofilm-related business opportunities best fit D.Med's capabilities and strategic direction?_

**Opportunities evaluated:**

1. Real-Time Biofilm Sensors (In-Line)
2. Integrated Biofilm Management Platform (SaaS)
3. Continuous Water Quality Monitoring Systems
4. Automated Compliance Reporting (ISO 23500)
5. Predictive Analytics for Biofilm Growth (AI/ML)

{% hint style="info" %}
**Evidence quality flag:** The scores below are structured expert-judgment estimates by the thesis author, informed by Chapters 2–6, not validated primary data. They should be triangulated with stakeholder interviews before being presented as final findings.
{% endhint %}

## 7.2 Evaluation Methodology Recap

### 7.2.1 Scoring Scale (1–5, all dimensions)

| Score | Market Attractiveness                                     | Technical Feasibility                  | Regulatory Complexity (Ch. 5)              | Strategic Fit                                       |
| ----: | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **5** | €100M+ SOM, >20% CAGR, severe pain point, low competition | TRL 8–9, off-shelf, simple integration | Class I, no clinical data, <6mo approval   | Excellent alignment, low resources, <12mo to market |
| **4** | €50–100M, 10–20% CAGR                                     | TRL 7–8, proven tech                   | Class I/IIa, literature CER, 6–12mo        | Good alignment, 12–18mo                             |
| **3** | €20–50M, 5–10% CAGR                                       | TRL 6–7, emerging tech                 | Class IIa, pilot study, 12–18mo            | Moderate, 18–24mo                                   |
| **2** | €5–20M, <5% CAGR                                          | TRL 5–6, unproven                      | Class IIb, clinical investigation, 18–36mo | Poor, 24–36mo                                       |
| **1** | <€5M, negative growth                                     | TRL <5, R\&D stage                     | Class III, full PMA, >36mo                 | Very poor, >36mo                                    |

### 7.2.2 Overall Score Formula

> **Overall Score = (Market Attractiveness × 0.35) + (Technical Feasibility × 0.25) + (Regulatory Complexity × 0.20) + (Strategic Fit × 0.20)**

## 7.3 Opportunity 1 — Real-Time Biofilm Sensors (In-Line)

| Dimension             | Sub-Score | Rationale (Summary)                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | --------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Market Attractiveness |   **4.5** | Severe pain point, high willingness to pay, no reimbursement barrier (Chapter 5, Section 5.8.5); moderate competition (BioDetect, Aquatell) |
| Technical Feasibility |   **3.4** | Mixed TRL across sensor types (5–8); moderate integration complexity; some IP uncertainty                                                   |
| Regulatory Complexity |   **3.3** | Class IIa/IIb (Chapter 5, Section 5.3.1); pilot validation study likely required (Section 5.6.3, Option 3); 12–18 month approval            |

## 7.9 Sensitivity Analysis

Four scenarios were tested to check ranking robustness:

| Scenario                                  | Assumption                                                                       | Result                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Optimistic**                         | Market sizes +50%, CAGR +5pp                                                     | Ranking unchanged; #1 and #2 unchanged                                           |
| **2. Pessimistic**                        | Market sizes −50%, CAGR −5pp                                                     | Ranking unchanged; #1 and #2 unchanged                                           |
| **3. Stricter MDR enforcement**           | Regulatory complexity score −1.0 for all (Chapter 5, Section 5.9.2 risk factors) | Ranking unchanged; software still leads due to inherently lower regulatory class |
| **4. D.Med acquires software capability** | Strategic Fit +1.0 for software opportunities (2, 4, 5)                          | Ranking unchanged; gap between software and hardware _widens_                    |

**Conclusion:** the ranking is **robust** across all tested scenarios. Automated Compliance Reporting and the Integrated Biofilm Platform remain the top two opportunities regardless of market, regulatory, or capability assumptions.
